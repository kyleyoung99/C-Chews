Savour

Three complete landing page templates for the same product and offer — the 30-second chew built to erase coffee and wine residue, not mask it. Same belief chain, same nine objections, radically different execution.

Prices are controlled from a single file: /shared/config.js. Change one number there and all three templates update.

Template 01

The Mechanism

For: cold, skeptical, problem-aware DR traffic

Long-form direct response. High-contrast editorial layout, one accent color, and the highest word count of the three — built to win a skeptical reader on argument and mechanism, section by section, culminating in a full comparison table and objection FAQ.

Savour

Three landing page templates for the same product and offer — the 30-second chew built to erase coffee and wine residue, not mask it. Each is a close structural reproduction of a proven DTC landing page pattern, rebuilt with Savour's own product, copy, and design tokens.

Prices are controlled from a single file: /shared/config.js. Change one number there and all three templates update.

Template 01 · modeled on AG1

The Subscription Case

For: cold, skeptical, higher-intent traffic

Calm and declarative, with one accent color doing all the work. Collapses "mints, gum, a whitening pen, mouthwash" into one argument — one chew replaces the drawer — then makes the subscription the obviously correct choice.

The Flavor Shelf

For: warm and social traffic

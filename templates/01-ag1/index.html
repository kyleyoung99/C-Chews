Why Savour

One chew replaces the drawer.

What’s inside

Three parts, one chew.

The formula is short on purpose. Three jobs, one tablet, nothing you need a dictionary to read.

The cooling agent

Erythritol

An endothermic melt cools the mouth, which is what closes enamel’s pores while residue is still fresh.

Full ingredient detail

Erythritol is a food-grade sugar alcohol used here for its cooling heat of solution, not for sweetness. Full dosage and sourcing will be published on the tin and here before Batch 001 ships.

The acid that washes

Malic acid

Malic acid triggers a salivary flood that carries coffee oils and wine pigments away before they bond. Your own saliva is the delivery system, not a synthetic rinse.

Full ingredient detail

Malic acid occurs naturally in apples and is used here at food-grade levels to stimulate salivary flow. Full concentrations and sourcing will be published here before Batch 001 ships.

The flavor system

Matched to the drink

Cardamom and sweet cream after coffee. Dark cherry and sage after wine. Designed to sit with what you just drank instead of fighting it the way mint does.

Full ingredient detail

Flavors are built from food-grade natural flavor systems, with no added colors. The full declaration, including allergen statements, will appear on the tin and here before the first order ships.

How it works

Snap, melt, wash.

  1. 1

    Snap

    You bite once. The shell cracks and releases the actives while residue is freshest.

  2. 2

    Melt

    The erythritol melt cools the mouth, which is what closes enamel’s pores.

  3. 3

    Wash

    A malic-acid-driven salivary flood carries oils and pigments away, then you swallow it.

About thirty seconds, start to finish. Nothing to spit, no sink required.

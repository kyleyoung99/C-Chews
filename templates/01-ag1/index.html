What’s inside

Three parts, one chew.

The formula is short on purpose. Three jobs, one tablet, nothing you need a dictionary to read.

The cooling agent

Erythritol

An endothermic melt cools the mouth and is designed to help close enamel’s pores while residue is still fresh. [SOURCE NEEDED — dental/chemistry citation]

Full ingredient detail

Erythritol is a food-grade sugar alcohol used here for its cooling heat of solution rather than for sweetness. Amounts, sourcing, and the mechanism reference will be published in full before launch. [SOURCE NEEDED — dental/chemistry citation]

The acid that washes

Malic acid

Formulated to prompt a salivary flood that helps carry coffee oils and wine pigments away before they bond. Your own saliva is the delivery system, not a synthetic rinse. [SOURCE NEEDED — dental/chemistry citation]

Full ingredient detail

Malic acid occurs naturally in apples and is used at food-grade levels to stimulate salivary flow. Concentrations and the supporting chemistry will be cited here rather than paraphrased. [SOURCE NEEDED — dental/chemistry citation]

The flavor system

Matched to the drink

Cardamom and sweet cream after coffee. Dark cherry and sage after wine. Designed to sit with what you just drank instead of fighting it the way mint does.

Full ingredient detail

Flavors are built from food-grade natural flavor systems, with no added colors. The full declaration, including allergen statements, will appear on the tin and here before the first order ships.
